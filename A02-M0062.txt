Frozen tuple
D — Exactly two reported oxygen-isotope exponent records from La₂₋ₓBaₓCuO₄ at x = 1/8 in the same published isotope-exchange study: the static spin-stripe ordering temperature Tₛₒ and the superconducting transition temperature T꜀. Excluded: Vₘ, other observables, other compositions, pressure variation, other studies, and all unreported/raw measurements.
W — The reported oxygen-isotope exponent αO for the typed observable.
π — (material, x, isotope intervention, source study, observable/probe) ↦ (material, x, isotope intervention, source study). The projection deliberately deletes observable/probe identity.
dY — Absolute distance on the real exponent line: dY(a,b) = |a − b|.
ε — 0.00 exponent units. This detection-only gate asks whether the two reported uncertainty intervals are disjoint; it does not declare a practical-effect threshold.
Same-fiber rule E — Two observations are assigned to the same empirical fiber only when compound, nominal Ba concentration x = 1/8, ¹⁶O/¹⁸O intervention, and source study/sample family match exactly. Observable and probe labels are intentionally ignored. No cross-study pooling, approximate-composition binning, or post-hoc regrouping is allowed.
Measurement uncertainty — The parent record reports αTₛₒ = −0.57(6) and αT꜀ = +0.46(6). The parenthetical (6) is used only as the symmetric reported ±0.06 interval. No confidence level, independence assumption, or Gaussian combination is added.
Coverage rule — PASS only if both predeclared witness records are present, both carry numerical uncertainty, and a pairwise lower bound can be computed without cross-study pooling. This coverage rule authorizes RETAIN through a located counterexample only. It cannot authorize DESCENT_CANDIDATE or empirical cleanliness.
