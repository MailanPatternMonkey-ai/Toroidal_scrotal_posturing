Executive synthesis
CONTROLLING VERDICT / A real output-structure finding survived; the proposed rotating-lattice mechanism did not; and the packet never contained the state records needed to test an autonomous residual law.

The strongest combined finding is narrow but solid. In an inspected native voice record, Checking can be committed as a standalone assistant message before a different substantive answer in the same turn. The same packet also contains progressive-prefix controls, allowing those marker-first pairs to be distinguished from ordinary streaming. [S5-S7]
That architecture does not make Checking a retrieval witness, failure proxy, or demonstrated gate. In Funfunaudit, 67 of 71 Checking-containing turns lacked a tool record or visible search operation. In the frozen response ledger, the marked composite-failure rate was 19/125 (15.2%) versus 129/281 (45.9%) when unmarked; the case-stratified Mantel-Haenszel odds ratio was 0.3213. The association is retrospective and descriptive, but its direction blocks a generic marker-equals-failure reading. [S5, S8]
The geometry program gives a different result. The initial output create-time signal was fragile, the apparent 21-call holdout contained four exact re-shares of older calls, and every lane in the frozen 72-model bank failed its stop rule after deduplication. One later-cohort q=39 missing-output association remains worth prospectively retesting, but it reverses the older half, vanishes in all 40 unique calls, and occurs at a one-opportunity-lagged arriving transition rather than the source-defined departing transition. [S1-S3]
The return-residual review closes the conceptual loop. Most later quotient maps are reconstructible because their preimage ledgers survive. The hydrated source payload does not. More importantly, the packet lacks predeclared loop IDs, typed state comparators, and repeated equal-state transitions, so residual descent is UNRESOLVED_NOT_TESTED - not demonstrated and not refuted. 2. What survives the behavioral audit
2.1 Native marker/answer separation
Funfunaudit contains 305 assistant voice messages in 223 turn groups. Of 82 two-message groups, 52 marker-led pairs contain a marker followed by a different substantive answer; none is a progressive-prefix duplicate. Four non-marker two-message turns do behave as progressive prefixes and therefore provide a local streaming control. [S5-S7]
Mentioning Something Important provides the counter-witness: its two two-message responses are progressive extensions. The finding is therefore not 'all multi-message turns are interposition'; it is that native records can distinguish interposed marker messages from progressive streaming when message and turn IDs survive. [S5-S7]
2.2 What Checking does not identify
- Retrieval: most audited Checking turns have no visible tool or search operation.
- Failure: marked responses have a lower coded composite-failure rate in the frozen sample; the result is descriptive, not confirmatory.
- A gate: no supplied test measures routing, admissibility, refusal, tool access, or answer preservation conditional on the marker while controlling comparable turns.
- An actor: output recurrence does not identify a person, office, council, motive, or conscious state.
- A universal lexical count: exact, starts-with, and contains-Checking rules produce different counts and answer different questions.
2.3 Correction, redaction, and the five labels
The packet supports local 'correction without control' episodes: a response can diagnose or acknowledge a failure without the named correction governing the next output. The immediate Funfunaudit specimen is 0/1 correction binding, which is an event report rather than an estimated rate. [S5-S7]
Redacted tool slots establish record opacity. They do not establish the missing payload, selective censorship, motive, or no tool use. Wrapper-created redactions belong to the wrapper and must not be added to the source-call total. [S5-S7]
FIVE-FUNCTION STATUS / Intercessor, Whisper, Custodian, Balancer, and Defendant Mirror remain a useful coding hypothesis. The supplied packet does not contain a frozen opportunity denominator, transition matrix, reliability pass, or matched negative set establishing an ordered five-stage workflow.
